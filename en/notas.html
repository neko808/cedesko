Today We celebrate 132 years of Coca-Cola!


On May 8, 1886 in Atlanta, Georgia, Dr. John Stith Pemberton, wanting to find a formula that would cure digestion problems and provide energy, accidentally mixed his medicinal syrup with carbonated water and then walked down the street with a jug of the new product to Jacobs' Pharmacy where it was tasted by the people who rated it as "excellent". Subsequently, the glass was put up for sale in a soda fountain for five cents.

At the time Pemberton and his accountant Frank Robinson, they realized that the creation could become a great success, so they are associated. Frank, who believed that "the two Cs would look good in advertising", suggested the name and wrote it: "Coca-Cola" with his unique calligraphy.


It was 13 years after its creation in an Atlanta pharmacy that agreements were signed to bottle Coca-Cola throughout the United States and this was the birth of the base of the global business model of the company: Coca-Cola supplied the concentrate while the authorized bottlers produced the beverage, distributed it and marketed it.

Did you know that in 1915 a contest was held to achieve a single bottle model for Coca-Cola which would be common among the existing bottlers and the following ones?

The design that turned out to be the winner was that of Alexander Samuelson, with his now famous "bottle Contour" (that's his official name) which to this day and next to the logo of Coca-Cola are the most recognized icons of the company throughout the world.


Today, The Coca-Cola Company manages hundreds of beverage brands around the world. In the case of The Mexican Coca-Cola Industry as a Total Beverage Company, we have almost 70 brands in 10 different categories; In addition, 45% of our portfolio has low or no calorie options and we continue to invest in the preparation of new recipes through the Coca-Cola Innovation and Development Center.

Growing our business is part of the discipline, says The Coca-Cola Company



Coca-Cola is accelerating its transition to a Total Beverage Company and, for this, has renewed its focus on innovation and growth, according to James Quincey, President and CEO of The Coca-Cola Company, before more than 120 investors and financial analysts who met at the company's headquarters to participate in Investor Day 2017.

At the first meeting of its kind held since 2009, Quincey and other senior leaders of the company explained how the company is expanding its product portfolio by placing the consumer at the center of the strategy. In addition, triumphs have been established in different markets and adopting an experimental attitude of trial and error.

Example of the above is the next launch that the company will make in Europe of the soy drink AdeS, which in Latin America has a great acceptance. Also Honest Tea and Smartwater recently "jumped the puddle", from the USA. to the United Kingdom.

Cinemex and Mexican Coca-Cola industry announce Trade Alliance



Cinemex announces its commercial alliance with the Mexican Coca-Cola Industry (IMCC), as its new beverage supplier for its more than 2,760 screens in 93 cities throughout the country.

Cinemex and the Mexican Coca-Cola Industry come together to meet the different needs and preferences of the guests, offering a wide range of drinks with low calorie options. With the entry into force of this alliance, both companies will carry out joint marketing and image activities.

The change will be made gradually so that as of April of this year, all the Cinemex complexes in the country offer more than 15 products from the IMCC portfolio, including Coca-Cola Sin Azúcar, Coca-Cola Original, Coca-Cola Light , Ciel, Ciel Exprim, Fuze Tea, Sprite, Fanta and Sidral Mundet.
