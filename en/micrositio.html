Arca Continental

www.arcacontal.com

Arca Continental is a company dedicated to the production, distribution and sale of non-alcoholic beverages owned by The Coca-Cola Company, as well as salted snacks under the brands Bokados in Mexico, Inalecsa in Ecuador and Wise in the United States.


With an outstanding history of more than 91 years, Arca Continental is the second largest bottler of Coca-Cola in Latin America and one of the most important in the world. In its Coca-Cola franchise, the company serves a population of over 118 million in northern and western Mexico, as well as Ecuador, Peru, northern Argentina and the southwestern United States. Arca Continental is listed on the Mexican Stock Exchange under the symbol "AC".

Coca-Cola Femsa KOF

www.coca-colafemsa.com

Coca-Cola FEMSA was born in 1993, as a result of the partnership between FEMSA and The Coca-Cola Company.


With the acquisition of the largest franchise of the Coca-Cola system in Latin America (PANAMCO) in May 2003, Coca-Cola FEMSA acquires presence in nine countries: Mexico, Guatemala, Nicaragua, Costa Rica, Panama, Venezuela, Colombia, Argentina, Brazil.


In January 2007, in partnership with The Coca Cola Company, it acquired Jugos del Valle (JDV), consolidating its presence in the non-carbonated beverages market in Mexico and Brazil. Between 2010 and 2012, it integrates four large Mexican business groups: Grupo Tampico, CIMSA, Fomento Queretano and Grupo Yoli, in addition to the acquisition of Santa Clara.


In 2013 he decided to venture into Asia, acquiring a 51% stake in Coca-Cola Bottlers Philippines Inc., adding his tenth territory.


Currently, Coca-Cola FEMSA is the largest bottler and beverage marketer in the world by sales volume within the Coca-Cola System, with the purpose of generating economic, social and environmental value in all its territories. It represents 14% of the overall sales volume of the Coca-Cola System.


In Mexico, it has a presence in 14 states: Mexico City, Mexico City, Puebla, Morelos, Tlaxcala, Querétaro Guanajuato, Michoacán, Veracruz, Guerrero, Tampico, Oaxaca, Chiapas and Tabasco. It has 24 production plants, 145 distribution centers, more than 850 thousand points of sale, serves 71.1 million consumers and offers employment to more than 43 thousand employees, being the largest bottler in the country.


The company is a member of the Dow Jones Emerging Markets Sustainability Index, FTSE4Good Emerging Index, IPC and Social Responsibility and Sustainability indexes of the Mexican Stock Exchange, among others.


Mission

Satisfy and please beverage consumers with excellence.

View

To be the best global enterprise in leading beverage brand sales. To generate sustainable economic and social value by managing innovative and winning business models with the best employees in the world.

Values

  • Passion for customer service
  • Respect and integral development of employees
  • Integrity and austerity
  • Creation of social value
  • Trust

Embotelladora de Colima

www.embotelladoradecolima.com

Coca-Cola reaches Colima in 1942 thanks to the initiative and leadership of the family Brun, which initiated the distribution and sales of the Coca-Cola product 6.5 Oz. This product was shipped from Embotelladora “La Favorita” in Guadalajara, Jalisco by rail.

In 1954 the construction the bottling plant “Embotelladora de Colima” started. The company was founded by Mr. Emilio Castellanos O, Eduardo Brun S, Guillermo Brun R, Carlos Garcia Castellanos, Juan Garcia Castellanos and Vicente Garcia Castellanos. In April 1955 Embotelladora de Colima starts operations with a production line that had a capacity of 36 bottles per minute and 37 employees.

It was the first plant in Latin America that bottled Fanta Naranja and thus the second one that released to the Mexican market.

The franchise “Embotelladora de Colima” currently comprises the south region of Jalisco and the Colima region, it has 2 production and distribution centers within the city of Colima, it also has 2 lines of carbonated products and 1 line Agua Ciel water in its jug presentation, as well as 4 distribution centers in Ciudad Guzman, Tamazula, Sayula and Pihuamo, generating sources of direct employment for more than 1,000 employees and it provides service and a supply network for 10555 customers.

Embotelladora de Colima has always stood out for its commitment to product quality and good service always rating in the first places of the National Quality Index, gaining recognition as the most recent 1st place in the National Cup Run 2009, achieving certification in the Coca-Cola Quality System and now moving forward towards the certification in the ISO Management Systems, all thanks to the leadership of its management and the commitment of its employees in achieving the mission and vision as a company.

View

PetStar

www.petstar.mx

PetStar is a sustainability project that defines the commitment of the Mexican Coca-Cola Industry to the environment. Its investors are: Arca Continental, Coca-Cola de México, Bepensa Bebidas, Corporación del Fuerte, Corporación Rica, Grupo Embotellador Nayar and Embotelladora de Colima. In June 2013 PetStar was named the World's Largest PET Food Grade Recycling Facility by "PCI PET Packaging Resin and Recycling, Ltd." with a capacity to produce 50,000 tonnes of high-quality recycled PET resin with a Social responsibility approach.

PetStar has a philosophy of excellence backed by certifications in ISO9001, ISO14001, ISO22000, ISO 50001, OHSAS18001, Safe Enterprise, Clean Industry, Clean Transport, Socially Responsible Enterprise, Super Companies, Operation Clean Sweap, as well as adherence to the Global Compact and Earth Charter.


Mission

  • Promote the preservation of the environment for the benefit of future generations, by implementing innovative solutions that make the PET packaging sustainable, which we collect and recycle efficiently, to produce high quality food grade resin with social responsibility.

View

  • To be a worldwide reference for excellence through the inclusive recycling of PET packaging, which with talent, commitment, innovation and well-being, we bring value to the social and environmental responsibility of our customers, contributing to be a solution to climate change.

Values

Imer

In 2005, as a result of the alliance with Coca Cola Mexico, Coca Cola Femsa and Alpla de México was born the Mexican Recycling Industry, it is the first food grade recycling plant in Mexico and Latin America.

For the construction of the company, recycling technology capable of transforming post-consumer PET into food-grade flake was chosen, through a chemical reaction and physical processes that allow the total decontamination of the material, this process guarantees the highest characteristics of Purity comparable to those of virgin material, which nowadays allow millions of bottles to include up to 50% recycled material.

Imer has processed more than 140,000 tons in 10 years and currently has a capacity to process 25,000 tons of pet per year, has the backing of the health secretary and contributes significantly to the conservation of our environment and environment.
